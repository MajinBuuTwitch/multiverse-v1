FINDINGS (every miss, reported, none patched)
==========================================================================================
F1 · Ω_bc error bar too small in the model-true universes (the one that looks real)
  C2's Law 1 Ω_bc means are centred on the truth (bias −0.0009 to −0.0043) but scatter 1.8–1.9× wider than the quoted
  posterior σ (χ² of 14.7 and 16.9 for 5 realizations; p ≈ 0.01 and 0.005). Same universes, so the four runs are not
  independent: this is one flag at about p ≈ 0.01, among roughly 25 checks, so chance can't be ruled out. C1 and C3
  show no such excess (ratios 0.5–1.0). In T2-A's own chain the excess shrinks to 1.25–1.36×. Cause not identified;
  five draws can't separate candidates. A v2 injection run should use more realizations per truth.

F1b · T2-A leans slightly toward FAIL when the model is true
  C2's T2-A tensions average −0.89σ (Reading B) and −0.61σ (Reading A), sd 1.02, about 2 standard errors below zero.
  This is the same direction as the real-data FAIL, so it is disclosed prominently. Its size does not change the
  verdict: real runs sit at −3.2 to −3.7σ, 2.3–2.8 sd beyond the model-true distribution even using its own widened
  scatter; the Planck-ΛCDM mocks (C1) give −3.29σ (B) and −2.97σ (A), sd 0.52, which is where the real data sit.

F2 · Spec flaw (mine): a truth on the prior edge
  The registered prior is γ_u ∈ [0, 2] and the 95% interval is [p2.5, p97.5], so the lower edge is always above zero
  (observed 0.0007–0.0047). "γ_u's 95% interval includes 0" could never be met: 0/5 by construction. The same edge
  pushes γ_u's mean positive (+0.02 to +0.05) and, through degeneracy, pulls h down and 100ω_b up by ~1.0–1.3σ.
  Not a pipeline fault. Non-patch context: every C1 γ_u posterior is within ~1.9σ of zero and C1's T1-L1 never PASSes.

F3 · Spec flaw (mine): H1 was a coin flip
  T6 FAILs when p_het > 0.3173. With no real variation, p_het is uniform, so FAIL happens 68% of the time by
  construction and "≥4 of 5 FAIL" happens only ~49% of the time for a perfect pipeline. Observed p_het 0.74, 0.24,
  0.30, 0.71, 0.89 (consistent with uniform). The two non-FAILs were INCONCLUSIVE: zero false detections.

F4 · T1-Sign power at w0 = −0.90
  Reading A gave 3/5 (DES-Dovekie P(w0 > −1) of 0.90–0.95, just under the 0.95 line). Reading B 4/5. The test sits
  at its power edge for a 10% departure. Real data reached 99.6–99.9%, far beyond. Related: ΛCDM mocks never
  produced a false T1-Sign PASS (all 10 INCONCLUSIVE), though they reached P(w0 > −1) of 0.35–0.93.

F5 · CPL projection lean on a ΛCDM sky
  For a ΛCDM truth, CPL posterior means land near w0 ≈ −0.95, wa ≈ −0.18 (about 0.9σ), toward DESI's quadrant: a
  prior-volume effect (wa ≥ −3, w0 + wa < 0). One cell crosses the line (1.05σ). Worth knowing for anyone reading CPL
  numbers from this pipeline.

F6 · Regional H0
  One cell misses (H3 region 0). H2/H3 regional values run +0.6 to +0.9 km/s/Mpc high in common, traced to the
  calibrator draw (mean fitted M_B −19.236 over 15 universes vs truth −19.253, about 1.7 SE): consistent with chance
  and covered by the total σ. The Hubble-flow-only σ undercovers badly, as expected, because it omits the shared
  calibrator; the total σ is the right one. The H3 INCONCLUSIVE came from a dim calibrator draw (M_B −19.31) that
  dropped Z_all-sky to 2.98.
