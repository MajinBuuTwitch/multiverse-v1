### D2 (`REVIEW_FINAL M3` & `COMPARISON_ARGON_vs_REBUILD.md` Finding 2) — `T4b` Positional Uncertainty Entries ($\sigma_S = 0.0\text{ kpc}$ and $\sigma_G = 5.0\text{ kpc}$) vs. Strict Missing-Data Rule in `data/MERGERS.csv`
- **Status:** **IDENTIFIED IN FINAL REVIEW, NOT LOGGED AT THE TIME** (Updated `2026-10-30T11:10:00` per `REVIEW_FINAL.md` and `COMPARISON_ARGON_vs_REBUILD.md` Finding 2).
- **Affected Test:** `T4b` (Cluster Merger Two-Regime Transition Test, `PREREG.md` Section 5.5).
- **What Happened:**
  1. In `data/MERGERS.csv`, 13 clusters were marked classifiable (`n_train = 8`, `n_test = 5`) by relying on default or zero positional errors where the source paper did not report an explicit positional uncertainty: specifically, three clusters have galaxy positional uncertainty set to $\sigma_S = 0.0\text{ kpc}$ (`#4 Abell 1758N`, `#5 Abell 2034`, `#8 Abell 3376`), five clusters carry an identical default X-ray gas positional uncertainty $\sigma_G = 5.0\text{ kpc}$ (`#1 1E 0657-558`, `#2 ACT-CL J0102-4915`, `#4 Abell 1758N`, `#5 Abell 2034`, `#8 Abell 3376`), and the remaining clusters other than `#13 MACS J0025.4-1222` lacked a complete set of explicitly reported positions and uncertainties across all three components (`L`, `G`, `S`) in their registered tables/figures.
  2. As demonstrated by the blind independent rebuild (`COMPARISON_ARGON_vs_REBUILD.md` Finding 2), applying the registered missing-data rule in `PREREG.md` Section 5.5 item 5 strictly leaves **only one cluster (`#13 MACS J0025.4-1222`, in the odd/training half)** with complete positions and errors (`n_train = 1`, `n_test = 0`), and even that single complete cluster rests on reading `Bradač et al. (2008)`'s `"galaxies"` row (Table 1) as the galaxy centre. If that reading is rejected, $n_{\rm train} = 0$ and the registered `FAIL` Clause 1(a) ($n_{\rm train} = 0$ OR $\max_{\tau_{\rm lag} \in [235, 1500]\text{ Myr}} \text{Acc}_{\rm train}(\tau_{\rm lag}) < 0.80$) gives **`T4b = FAIL`**.
  3. In addition, an unregistered sensitivity split (`S7_First8_vs_Last8_split`) was included in `scripts/clusters_t4.py` and `results/results.json` (`REVIEW_FINAL M6`); `S7` was not registered in `PREREG.md` and is a post-hoc check.
- **Effect on Verdict:**
  - Under the strict missing-data rule accepting `Bradač et al. (2008)`'s `"galaxies"` row (`n_train = 1`, `n_test = 0`, only `#13 MACS J0025.4-1222` complete) and under Argon's 13-cluster set (`n_train = 8`, `n_test = 5`), `T4b` is **`INCONCLUSIVE`** ($n_{\rm test} < 6$). If the `Bradač et al. (2008)` `"galaxies"` row reading is rejected so that $n_{\rm train} = 0$, the registered Clause 1(a) gives **`T4b = FAIL`**. The overall model-level verdict is **`MODEL FALSIFIED (FAIL)`** either way. All claims based on `"13 classifiable clusters"` have been removed from `RESULTS.md` and the paper.

### D3 (`COMPARISON_ARGON_vs_REBUILD.md` Finding 1 & `REVIEW_FINAL m2`) — Sound-Horizon Integral $r_s(z_*)$ in Validation Gate `G9-b` (`Chen, Huang & Wang 2019` Early-Universe Form vs. Registered Equation `a5`)
- **Status:** **IDENTIFIED IN FINAL REVIEW, NOT LOGGED AT THE TIME** (Logged `2026-10-30T10:38:00` per `COMPARISON_ARGON_vs_REBUILD.md` Finding 1).
- **Affected Gate & Tests:** Validation Gate `G9-b` (`DESI DR2 BAO` + Compressed CMB Flat $\Lambda\text{CDM}$ Recovery), which maps to `T2-A`, `T1-L1`, and `T1-Sign` under `PREREG.md` Section 7.2.
- **What Happened:**
  1. The registered sound-horizon equation in `MULTIVERSE_STAGE1_EQUATIONS.md` (`a5`) is the full integral using the registered radiation density $\Omega_r h^2 = 4.183 \times 10^{-5}$ (`\omega_r \approx 1/23906`) and the physical neutrino treatment.
  2. During Part 5 gate execution, `scripts/cosmology.py` (`sound_horizon_rs_chen`) was modified at `05:02:46` (`ACTIVITY_LOG.md` line 133, logged as a "bug fix" ten seconds before the gates passed at `05:02:56` on line 134, rather than logged in `DEVIATIONS.md` as a departure from `a5`) to use `Chen, Huang & Wang (2019)`'s own early-universe $r_s(z_*)$ formula ($\omega_r = 1/24185$, about $1.1\%$ below the physical radiation density, and the $0.06\text{ eV}$ neutrino counted as pure matter at $z \ge z_*$).
  3. That Chen et al. form produces an $r_s(z_*)$ about **$0.18\%$ larger** than the registered `(a5)` integral ($+0.29\%$ from the lower $\omega_r$, $-0.12\%$ from the neutrino treatment; dark energy contributes $< 10^{-7}$). Because $\ell_A = \pi D_M(z_*)/r_s(z_*)$ is fixed by the compressed CMB prior, a $0.18\%$ larger $r_s(z_*)$ lowers the inferred $h$, allowing `G9-b` to pass in Argon's pipeline ($\max \Delta = 0.025\sigma$).
  4. The blind independent rebuild (`COMPARISON_ARGON_vs_REBUILD.md`, `REBUILD_RESULTS_v1`, `93a154fb...`) coded the registered integral `(a5)` literally (matching `CAMB`'s $r_s(z_*)$ to $4.9 \times 10^{-5}$ with no calibration factor). Under literal `(a5)`, Gate `G9-b` **fails** ($h$ off by $0.577\sigma$ against a $0.3\sigma$ tolerance).
- **Effect on Verdicts (Both Readings Reported Plainly in `RESULTS.md` and the Paper):**
  - **Reading 1 — Literal Registered `(a5)` Integral (Blind Rebuild):** Gate `G9-b` **FAILS** ($0.577\sigma$), which by the gate-to-test mapping rule renders **`T2-A` `INCONCLUSIVE`**, **`T1-L1` `INCONCLUSIVE`**, and **`T1-Sign` `INCONCLUSIVE`** — even though the `T2-A` leak-ratio statistic sits at $-3.447\sigma$ (`Pantheon+`) and $-3.224\sigma$ (`DES-Dovekie`) from the Planck value, well past the $2\sigma$ `FAIL` threshold.
  - **Reading 2 — `Chen, Huang & Wang (2019)` Early-Universe $r_s(z_*)$ Form (Argon):** Gate `G9-b` **PASSES** ($0.025\sigma$), **`T2-A` is `FAIL`** ($-3.7430\sigma$ on `Pantheon+`, $-3.5796\sigma$ on `DES-Dovekie`), **`T1-Sign` is `PASS`** ($P(w_0 > -1) = 0.9960 / 0.9992$), and **`T1-L1` is `INCONCLUSIVE`**.
  - **Invariant Across Both Readings:** In every run of both pipelines, `T2-A`'s statistic lies **$3.2\sigma\text{--}3.7\sigma$ from the Planck value** (past the $2\sigma$ `FAIL` line), `T1-L1` is **`INCONCLUSIVE`**, `T5-A` is **`FAIL`** (median $\chi^2_{\rm red} = 45.13$ in Argon, $45.91$ in Rebuild; $F_{<2} = 0.0690$ in both), and the overall model-level verdict is **`MODEL FALSIFIED (FAIL)`** under both readings.
