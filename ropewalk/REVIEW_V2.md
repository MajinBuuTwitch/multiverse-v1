## 2. New problems introduced or left by the revision

### BLOCKER

**N1. T2 (pillar 2) has four unresolved forks.** (a) T2-A and T2-B produce different R_pred, and the PASS/FAIL rule never says which is compared with R_Planck. (b) σ_comb is "evaluated both" uncorrelated and overlap-corrected, with no rule for which decides. ρ_overlap "≈ +0.35, computed by Gaussian Jacobian propagation" is an unsourced, approximate number to be computed later, so it is an open choice. (c) Two BBN priors (Cooke+18 "primary", post-LUNA "updated") are registered, and §5.2 never says the other is reported only. (d) The T2-B paragraph says it "fails if Δχ² > 9.0", but the T2 FAIL rule omits that condition, so that case falls through to INCONCLUSIVE. The same ambiguity carries into T5, which "uses f_leak frozen from the Pantheon+ primary run" without naming T2-A or T2-B, or the BBN prior.
*Fix:* "Decisive T2 = T2-A, Cooke/DESI-BBN [pick one], σ_comb^corr with ρ_overlap = [number, source/chain named now]. All other variants are reported only. T2-B is a separate secondary test with its own PASS/FAIL. T5 uses f_leak from [exact run]."

**N2. T4b (pillar 3) has four unresolved forks.** (a) The τ_lag grid starts at "150 Myr (or 400 Myr under Dawson)", and FAIL clause (a) still says "no step ≥ 150 Myr". (b) The Bullet age is listed as 0.40 *(0.15 shock)*, with "both evaluated and reported" and no deciding value. (c) Hard-step accuracy and "age-posterior-weighted accuracy" are both computed, with no rule saying which is compared with the 80%/60% thresholds. (d) "Both subcluster-level and system-level classifications are reported", again with no deciding one. FAIL (c) also does not say whether "every classified system" means the test half or all 16.
*Fix:* name exactly one value for each item. I recommend Dawson 2013 temporal prior for the Bullet, a grid floor fixed to one number (see N8), hard-step accuracy deciding, subcluster level deciding, and FAIL (c) evaluated on the test half.

**N3. No precedence clause; hashed files point to the superseded test file.** M11 asked for "MAIN governs" in PREREG. Nothing in PREREG states precedence over MULTIVERSE_TESTS_v1.md, the PART files, or EQ on thresholds. PART2 (g4) still has 12 pixels with dof = 11. PREREG §1 says it incorporates the TESTS_v1 thresholds "without softening any threshold". That last change is a softening of the FAIL criterion, and it is undeclared.
*Fix:* add to PREREG §1: "PREREG.md v2 supersedes MULTIVERSE_TESTS_v1.md, GEMINI.md rule R12 and all STAGE1_PART files for every test definition, threshold and decision rule. EQ governs equations. ### MAJOR

**N5. T1-L1 decision rule and forecast.** (a) The outcome map is incomplete. A per-run result with A = Δχ²(L1−ΛCDM) ∈ (−4, −1] and B = Δχ²(L1−w0wa) ≤ 4 is neither PASS, FAIL nor INCONCLUSIVE. B ∈ (4, 9] with A > −1 is both FAIL and INCONCLUSIVE. "In both runs, X or Y" doesn't fix the grouping. Neither case can occur with the published improvements (w0wa − ΛCDM = −10.7 Pantheon+, −13.5 Dovekie), but compressed priors may shrink them. *Fix:* "evaluate per run; FAIL takes precedence; every per-run outcome not PASS or FAIL is INCONCLUSIVE". Also register the χ²_min optimizer. (b) Wrong numbers in the forecast. PREREG §5.1 says wa = −0.62 ± 0.22 (**DES-Dovekie**) to −0.86 ± 0.25 (**Pantheon+**). In fact −0.62 (+0.22/−0.19) is Pantheon+, −0.86 is the *original* DESY5, and Dovekie is wa = −0.72 ± 0.21. The T1-Sign note gives Dovekie w0 = −0.84 ± 0.06 (z = 2.67); the published value is −0.803 ± 0.054 (z = 3.65). (c) The forecast "Δχ² ≈ 7–12, FAIL" is inflated: B cannot exceed 10.7 for Pantheon+. My rough forecast gives B ≈ 7–8 (Pantheon+, INCONCLUSIVE band) and ≈ 9–10 (Dovekie). Under §3.3 the **likely joint outcome is INCONCLUSIVE**, not FAIL. EQ already says "FAIL or INCONCLUSIVE"; PREREG and the film text say FAIL. (d) Law 1 is not nested in w0waCDM, so "> 9 = 3σ for Δdof = 1" is not a valid likelihood-ratio reading. The number is still mechanical; drop the σ gloss.

**N6. Holm–Bonferroni (M10) cannot be applied mechanically.** No p-value is defined for T1-L1 (Δχ²), T4b (accuracy), T5-A/B (median χ²_red and fractions) or T5c. Nothing says what happens to a PASS that fails Holm. The model-level outcome when there is no pillar FAIL but at least one INCONCLUSIVE is not stated. *Fix:* define a p-value per scored test, or restrict Holm to tests that have one. State "a PASS failing Holm is reported as INCONCLUSIVE". Add a third model-level verdict, "not falsified, not supported".

**N7. M9 marked FIXED but not fixed in substance.** Cooke+18 (100ω_b = 2.166) remains the *primary* T2 BBN prior. Under the new conditional likelihood (R, ℓ_A | ω_b), Cooke's ω_b shifts the conditional R mean by C₁₂/C₂₂·(−0.070) = +0.014, which is 3.1σ_R. That is exactly the 3σ BBN–CMB conflict M9 said would bias the frozen Ω_bc and f_leak, which then feed T5. *Fix:* make DESI BBN (0.02218 ± 0.00055) primary and Cooke secondary, as REVIEW_COLD recommended, or argue the choice explicitly.

**N8. The "τ_lag ≥ 400 Myr" lower bound is a median, not a bound (M6).** Dawson 2013 Table 2 with the temporal prior gives TSC0 = 0.4 Gyr, 68%: 0.3–0.5, **95%: 0.2–0.6**. With default priors it gives 0.6 Gyr (0.3–1.1), and 0.6 is the median, not an upper end. Requiring t_p(Bullet) < τ_lag therefore gives τ_lag > 0.3 Gyr at 84% and > 0.2 Gyr at about 97.5%. Keeping the Markevitch 0.15 Gyr shock age as a parallel option is unsupported: Dawson §3.2.2 and Springel & Farrar 2007 show the shock speed overstates the subcluster speed. *Fix:* register the floor as a stated quantile of the Dawson temporal-prior posterior (e.g. one-sided 95%, about 0.25 Gyr) or as P(τ > t_p) ≥ 0.5 (0.40 Gyr), pick one, and drop 0.15 Gyr from the decision path.

**N9. Forks in scored secondary tests (in Holm K).** T5-B: §3.1 says the Bonferroni-adjusted thresholds (1.35 / 75%) are required, while §5.6 says they are "reported alongside" the raw ones. ("Bonferroni" on χ² cut-offs is also a misnomer.) T6: there are two covariances × two redshift frames × two coordinate systems, and only PASS says "both covariances". FAIL and INCONCLUSIVE use a single p_het without naming which one. FAIL is undefined when the all-sky tension is < 3σ. σ_CV,reg = 0.013·H̄0 is unsourced. T2-N2 has 3 ω_b values × 2 H0 references with none deciding. Its quoted "3.5–6.2σ" is wrong (I get 2.5σ for LUNA ω_b vs Planck, up to 6.8σ for Cooke vs DESI+BBN), and DESI+BBN H0 shares the BBN prior with the prediction.

**N10. M6(a) not addressed.** The 16 T4b systems are still hand-selected by an author who knew the outcomes. No algorithmic selection rule was added, yet the response marks M6 FIXED. *Fix:* adopt a rule, or state the hand selection plainly in PREREG as a known limitation.

## 4. "WHAT CHANGES FOR THE FILM" check

1. **"τ_lag ≥ 400 Myr ... up to 600 Myr without a timing prior": OVERSTATED.** 400 Myr is Dawson's median with the X-ray timing prior (68%: 300–500; 95%: 200–600). 600 Myr is the default-prior median (68%: 300–1,100), not an upper end. Accurate wording: "the lag most likely exceeds about 400 Myr, and almost certainly exceeds about 200–300 Myr."
2. **T4a exploratory, shadow trails for the first two-thirds: ACCURATE in substance.** The 2/3 sign switch holds for the chosen harmonic model, but that model is idealized for lags this long. "Only one collision under 150 Myr" is true, though the Dawson-era cut of 400 Myr would leave five, so the reason given for dropping T4a is weaker than the film text implies.
3. **Ω_b = 1/19 is 5.26% vs 4.93%, 6.8% higher, nearly 4σ; H0 ≈ 65: ACCURATE.** I get +6.76% and 3.87σ (uncorrelated propagation; a positive Planck ω_b–h correlation would make it larger). H0 = 65.19 ± 0.22, 3.7σ below Planck and 7.4σ below SH0ES. "T2 forecast to fail" is accurate, and if anything understated (3.8–3.9σ).
4. **T1-L1 curve flatter than DESI, forecast to fail: PARTLY OVERSTATED.** "Flatter" is right: tangent wa ≈ −0.10 vs −0.62 (Pantheon+) / −0.72 (Dovekie), which is roughly 6–7×, or about 4× using an effective-CPL fit. "Forecast to fail" is too strong. With published numbers the Pantheon+ run most likely lands in the INCONCLUSIVE band, which makes the joint result INCONCLUSIVE. Say "disfavoured; FAIL or INCONCLUSIVE".
5. **6:1 cap vs SPARC dwarfs at 10–30:1: SLIGHTLY OVERSTATED at the top end.** The cap of 6 and the "previously negative mass" claim are correct. The RAR gives 11.5:1 at g_bar = 10⁻¹² and about 16–20:1 at 10^−12.3 to 10^−12.5. 30:1 would need g_bar ≈ 10⁻¹³, below nearly all SPARC points. Say "10–20:1". "Forecast to fail the 70% bar" is fair as "FAIL or INCONCLUSIVE" (EQ's own wording).
6. **"Forecast to fail all four primary pillar tests": OVERSTATED.** PREREG §3.2 itself names only three (T1-L1, T2, T5-A). T4b has no reliable forecast: its own forecast hinges on the "disputed" Abell 520 core, plus forks N2 and N8. T1-L1 more likely ends INCONCLUSIVE. The defensible film line is: "v1 is forecast to be falsified, because T2 alone is about 3.8σ from passing and any single pillar FAIL falsifies the model. Two or three pillars are expected to fail or come out inconclusive." The DF2 parenthetical "blur wider than 3.3 kpc" should read about 2.2 kpc.
