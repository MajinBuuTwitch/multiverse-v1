1. THE STANDARD MACHINERY (T1) [Std]
==========================================================================================
Expansion rate (Friedmann):        H² = (8πG/3)(ρ_m + ρ_r + ρ_de)          E(a) ≡ H(a)/H_0
Acceleration:                      ä/a = −(4πG/3) Σ (ρ + 3p)
Each ingredient's bookkeeping:     ρ̇ + 3H(1 + w)ρ = 0,  w ≡ p/ρ,  so ρ ∝ a^−3(1+w)
  matter w = 0 (ρ ∝ a^−3) · radiation w = 1/3 (ρ ∝ a^−4) · cosmological constant w = −1 (constant)
  w < −1 ("phantom"): density GROWS as space expands · w > −1: density falls.
A density that rises then falls must cross w = −1 exactly at its peak.

Key epochs (ΛCDM-like, Ω_m = 0.31) [C, Gem]:
  acceleration begins (q = 0, ä = 0):       z ≈ 0.65  (~6 billion years ago)
  dark energy overtakes matter:             z ≈ 0.3   (~3.5 billion years ago)
  expansion rate at cosmic noon (z ≈ 2):    about 3 × today's
  dark energy's share of everything at z = 2.33: about 6% (so data barely see dark energy at cosmic noon)

Two horizons [C correction of Gem]:
  comoving Hubble radius 1/(aH): grows while expansion slows, SHRINKS once it accelerates (flips at z ≈ 0.65)
  comoving event horizon r_eh(a) = ∫_a^∞ c da' / (a'² H(a')): shrinks at ALL times; the true "can ever be reached"
  boundary, but it depends on the whole future.

Inertia (the lag) [G idea; Std math]: the expansion rate behaves like a velocity. When the net force reverses, the
expansion slows first, stops (H = 0), then reverses: Chapter 1's thrown ball.

Negative energy and the crunch [Std]: in a flat universe, positive dark energy accelerates; zero leaves it coasting
forever; negative dark energy (with p = −ρ it pulls inward) can stop and reverse the expansion. Turnaround when
ρ_m + ρ_r + ρ_de = 0, i.e. negative dark energy cancels matter.

Time dilation (T1) [C]: distant supernovae and quasars run in slow motion by exactly (1 + z) (DES 2024 out to z ~ 1,
arXiv:2406.05050). A clock-rate effect tied to dark energy (design log Entry 7) would show as a drift from (1 + z).

    The stock rises while inflow beats outflow, peaks when they balance, falls after.
4b. Dark energy = tension minus the cohesion floor [G; C form]

    w_eff formally blows up where ρ_de passes through zero (a fingerprint of the sign change).
