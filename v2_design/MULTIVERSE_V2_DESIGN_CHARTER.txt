MULTIVERSE: VERSION 2 DESIGN CHARTER (the Mandarano Model v2)
Started 2026-10-31. Status: CHARTER ONLY. Nothing here is a v2 equation yet. This file sets the targets, the
rules and the plan for building v2 from what v1 taught us, so that v2 is built honestly and can still be
tested honestly.

==========================================================================================
0. THE IDEA (Gregory, 2026-10-31)
==========================================================================================
Before writing v2, take the data and conclusions from v1's tests and work out, with our own equations,
what a model would need in order to fit them. Design to the evidence, then lock and test.

THE ONE RULE THAT MAKES THIS LEGITIMATE
Building a model to fit data you've already seen is normal science, but that data can't then count as
evidence FOR the model. Everything v2 is fitted to is its DESIGN data. Only data v2 never touched during
design can test it. So the data v2 is NOT allowed to see must be chosen and fenced off BEFORE design work
starts (section 3).

==========================================================================================
1. WHAT V1 TAUGHT US (verified results; both pipelines agree)
==========================================================================================
- Galaxy rotation curves (T5-A): the decisive failure. One fixed blur of the visible matter fits 6 of 87
  held-out galaxies (median misfit ~45 vs a pass line of 1.5). The shadow can't simply be a blurred
  copy of the matter.
- The one-in-nineteen share: 5.26% vs Planck's 4.93% ± 0.09% (~3.9σ). The implied H0 (~65.2) is ~3.7σ
  below Planck.
- The leak ratio: 3.2–3.7σ from Planck.
- Dark energy: data favour w0 > −1 at ~99.6–99.9%, but the specific unwinding curve was neither confirmed
  nor ruled out. Head-to-head: on evidence, the free unwinding curve trails ΛCDM weakly to moderately
  (ln B −2.1 to −2.7; −0.7 to −1.5 with a narrower prior), about where the mainstream w0wa model sits.
  With one-in-nineteen imposed it is strongly disfavoured (ln B −6.9 to −8.5).
- Regional H0 (T6): no variation across 9 sky regions. The "local accent" idea should be dropped.
- NGC 1052-DF2: allowed, but only through the same wide blur that failed the rotation curves.
- Colliding-cluster lag: untestable with current data (too few clusters with complete measurements).
(Detail: MULTIVERSE_FOLLOWUP_SOURCE_CATALOGUE.txt, Parts 1 and 4; paper v5.)

2. THE TARGETS: what any v2 must reproduce (established measurements, Tier 1)
==========================================================================================
Each target gets its source and value verified before design work uses it.
 T-a  COSMIC SHARES: ordinary matter ~4.93%, dark matter ~26.5%, dark energy ~68.5% (Planck 2018).
      Dark-to-ordinary ratio 5.36 ± 0.07.
 T-b  THE RATIO HOLDS OVER COSMIC TIME: the same dark-to-ordinary ratio from the CMB era to today, with
      dark matter cold and pressureless early (CMB acoustic peaks, structure formation).
 T-c  GALAXIES: the radial acceleration relation. Observed acceleration tracks the visible matter's
      acceleration, with a characteristic scale near 1.2 × 10⁻¹⁰ m/s². Also the baryonic Tully–Fisher
      relation (visible mass scales with rotation speed to the 4th power). V1 failed here; THIS IS THE
      FIRST TARGET TO DESIGN AROUND.
 T-d  CLUSTERS: the Bullet Cluster's mass sits away from its hot gas, and clusters need more unseen mass
      than acceleration-based rules alone supply.
 T-e  GALAXIES WITH LITTLE DARK MATTER EXIST (DF2, DF4), without breaking T-c for ordinary galaxies.
 T-f  DARK ENERGY: current data lean toward weakening (w0 > −1), but evidence doesn't yet favour any
      extension over a constant.
 T-g  NO REGIONAL VARIATION IN THE EXPANSION RATE across the sky; the Hubble tension (local ~73 vs early
      ~67.4) remains real and unexplained.
 T-h  IF V2 CHANGES HOW GRAVITY WORKS: gravitational waves travel at the speed of light to about one part
      in 10¹⁵ (GW170817 with its light flash).

==========================================================================================
4. DESIGN RULES
==========================================================================================
 R1  KNOB BUDGET: list every free number. Predictions must outnumber knobs. Example: the three shares give
     only two independent numbers (they sum to 1), so a two-knob explanation of the shares predicts
     nothing.
 R2  DERIVE, DON'T FIT: a non-integer count (e.g. an effective ~20.3 strings from fray weight) is only
     science if it's calculated from the mechanism, the way the effective number of neutrino species
     3.044 is calculated, not chosen to match.
 R3  EVERY NEW MECHANISM MUST LEAVE A NEW FINGERPRINT: something measurable beyond the data it was built
     to explain (a scale dependence, an early-time difference, a galaxy-level signature).
 R4  LABEL EVERYTHING: design-phase fits are EXPLORATORY. The tier system applies: established, published
     speculation, the model.
 R5  NO RESCUE BY NUMEROLOGY: a number chosen after seeing the data (e.g. 1/20 because Planck says ~1/20.3)
     is a new hypothesis, never evidence.
 R6  SAME STANDARD AS V1 FOR THE TEST: lock, hash and stamp; two model families; blind rebuild; held-out
     data; injection tests.

   stamp.
