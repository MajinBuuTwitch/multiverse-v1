MULTIVERSE v2 · FENCED DATA LIST v1
Chosen by Gregory Mandarano, 2026-10-31. Recorded by the planning chat (Claude). Companion to
MULTIVERSE_V2_DESIGN_CHARTER.txt.

PURPOSE
These datasets are reserved to TEST version 2 of the Mandarano Model. They must not be analyzed, and their
results must not be read for design purposes, until v2's equations and pre-registration are locked
(charter Phases D/E). Everything v1 used (Planck 2018 / Chen+19 compressed CMB, DESI DR2 BAO, Pantheon+,
DES-Dovekie, SPARC, the 16-merger cluster set, the T6 supernova sample) is DESIGN data for v2.

FENCED
F1  Weak-lensing radial acceleration relation from KiDS-1000: Brouwer et al. 2021, A&A 650, A113
    (arXiv:2106.11677), and any later KiDS or other galaxy-galaxy-lensing extension of the same
    measurement.
F2  Rotation curves and mass models from outside SPARC, with every SPARC galaxy removed: the LITTLE THINGS
    dwarf sample (Oh et al. 2015) and MeerKAT MHONGOOSE mass models, plus later releases of either.
F3  Euclid's first cosmology results (DR1 cosmology products; expected mid-2027 at the earliest).
F4  The next DESI data release after DR2, and new CMB results such as from the Simons Observatory.

HONEST LIMIT
F1 and F2 are published, so their headline results may be in the training data of the AI models doing the
design work. The design team commits not to analyze them or consult them during design, but cannot promise
it has never heard of them. F3 and F4 did not exist when this list was stamped.
