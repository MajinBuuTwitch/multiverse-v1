1. TIER KEY, THE MODEL IN BRIEF, AND THE KNOB LEDGER
==========================================================================================
1.1 TIER KEY AND LABELS (charter R4).
    T1      Established measurement or standard physics.
    T2      Published speculation by others.
    MODEL   v2's own untested proposal.
    Labels used with them:
    RULE           A part of the model that is stated, not derived (for example, gravitational waves travel at c).
    [algebra]      A symbolic consequence of the frozen equations; no numerical model run.
    EXPLORATORY    A registered test that is run and reported but cannot decide anything.
    TENSION        The zone between a deciding test's pass and fail lines: reported in full, never sinks v2
                   (Entry 47 D8). It corresponds to v1's INCONCLUSIVE.
    REPORT-ONLY    Computed and shown, with no pass or fail.
    UNTESTABLE     Interpretation that no data can check (the "untestable bin"); never scored.

1.2 THE MODEL IN BRIEF (MODEL; the picture is Gregory's, the equations are §§2–4).
    - Dark energy is tension in a reservoir ρ_T. Interactions that leave lasting records, driven by star formation,
      wind loose frays (dark matter) into tension at a rate weighted by how much of the future remains causally
      reachable. Tension unwinds at a constant rate back into loose frays. Observable dark energy is the tension
      above a constant cohesion floor Λ_coh. The dark sector is closed: what tension gains, dark matter loses, and
      the reverse.
    - Galaxies feel extra pull from fresh branches of our own matter that stay in step with us briefly before
      fading. Their number scales as a₀/g, which gives an AQUAL (MOND-type) gravity law. a₀ is tied to the
      cohesion floor by a stated rule. Every bound system also carries a cored cloud of loose frays holding the
      cosmic dark-to-ordinary share.
    - The expansion eventually stops and reverses, and the collapse ends in a new cycle (UNTESTABLE).

2.1 UNITS AND FIXED BACKGROUND. H₀ = 1 and densities in units of today's critical density (Entry 29). Flat universe.
    E(a) ≡ H(a)/H₀, with E²(a) = Ω_r(a) + Ω_b(a) + Ω_ν(a) + Ω_dm(a) + Ω_de(a). Ordinary matter is conserved,
    Ω_b(a) = Ω_b,0 a⁻³. Cosmic time t = ∫ dln a / E, with 1/H₀ = 14.51 Gyr at h = 0.674 (Entry 29).   [Std]
    Radiation and neutrinos, two conventions (Entry 46 §5):
      Shape check (§2.7): Entry 29's frozen fixed background, Ω_r,0 = 9.2 × 10⁻⁵ (photons plus N_eff = 3.046
        relativistic species at h = 0.674), Ω_r(a) = Ω_r,0 a⁻⁴, no separate Ω_ν term.
      Evidence fits (§§3, 6): v1's convention, Ω_γ h² = 2.4728 × 10⁻⁵ (T_CMB = 2.7255 K), N_eff = 3.044 with two
        massless species and one of 0.06 eV, the massive one evaluated with v1's Fermi–Dirac integral f_ν(a)
        (Ω_ν,mass h² = 0.06/93.14 today).
      The difference shifts Ω_r,0 by about 5 × 10⁻⁴ of itself and r_s(z*) by under 10⁻⁴; it is disclosed.
2.2 THE TENSION RESERVOIR (MODEL; [G] stock idea, [Gem] equations, [C] checks; Entries 10, 39 §2):
      dρ_T/dt = −3H₀ γ_u ρ_T + Q(t)                                    (constant cosmic-time drain)
      ρ_de = ρ_T − Λ_coh,   Λ_coh > 0                                  (dark energy above the cohesion floor)
    The bare tension has w = −1. The effective equation of state of ρ_de is
      1 + w_eff = (3H₀ γ_u ρ_T − Q) / (3H ρ_de)                         [algebra]
    so w_eff < −1 while the reservoir fills, w_eff = −1 at its peak, w_eff > −1 after; w_eff is formally singular
    where ρ_de passes through zero.
2.3 THE PUMP (MODEL; Entries 11–13, 18, 20, 26, 39 §1):
      Q(t) = κ S(a) P(t),   κ ≥ 0
    - S(a) = ψ(z)/ψ(0), the cosmic star-formation history of Madau & Dickinson (2014, ARA&A 52, 415, eq. 15):
        ψ(z) = 0.015 (1 + z)^2.7 / {1 + [(1 + z)/2.9]^5.6}   M☉ yr⁻¹ Mpc⁻³                            [T1]
      Only the shape enters; the 0.015 normalisation is absorbed into κ. Peak at z = 1.86, peak/today = 8.9. For
      a > 1 the same formula continues with 1 + z = 1/a (S = 0.335 at a = 1.5, 0.154 at a = 2, 0.024 at a = 4).
      This future extension is an extrapolation of an empirical fit, not a measurement; it barely affects
      today's predictions but sets the predicted dates (§2.8), which carry this caveat.
    - Why every lasting interaction counts and why escape is bolometric: starlight absorbed and re-emitted by dust
      escapes into intergalactic space in the far infrared, so no wavelength band is singled out (Dole et al.
      2006; Madau & Dickinson 2014 §3; Driver et al. 2016). Choosing a sub-bolometric band would be a hidden dial
      (Entry 39 §1).
    - P(t), the forward causal reach: the odds that a record made now is ever answered by an absorber the
      forward-moving Now reaches before turnaround. With the universe transparent (τ_f ≪ 1), P ≈ τ_f, and the
      normalisation of τ_f is absorbed into κ. The convention both pipelines use (Entry 29):
        P(a) = ∫_a^{a_turn} a'⁻⁴ / E(a') da'   for t < t_turn,     P(t) = 0 for t ≥ t_turn
      P is defined by the remaining forward path in time, so it stays zero after turnaround even as a shrinks
      (Entry 26). Starvation (P) and activity (S) are full multiplicative partners: from z = 2 to today, S falls
      about 8.9 times and P about 12.9 times for ΛCDM-like expansion (Entry 39 §1).
    - Because P depends on the future expansion and the future on ρ_de, the model is solved as one loop (§2.6).
2.4 THE CLOSED DARK SECTOR (MODEL; Entry 39 §2): what tension gains, dark matter loses.
      ρ̇_dm + 3H ρ_dm = −Q(t) + 3H₀ γ_u ρ_T = −dρ_T/dt
    Total dark-sector energy is conserved locally (ρ̇_dm + ρ̇_de = −3H ρ_dm). Comoving dark matter obeys
      d(ρ_dm a³)/dt = −a³ [Q(t) − 3H₀ γ_u ρ_T(t)]                       [algebra]
    so it thins slightly faster than a⁻³ before the dark-energy peak, reaches a minimum at the peak, and thins
    slightly slower after it (Entry 39 §3). In H₀ = 1 units (Entry 39 §4):
      Ω_dm(a) a³ = Ω_dm,0 + ∫_a^1 a'³ [κ S(a') P(a') − 3γ_u Ω_T(a')] / E(a') dln a'
    Identity used throughout (Entry 43 §1): d(Ω_dm a³)/da = −a³ dΩ_de/da.                               [algebra]
2.5 STARTING CONDITIONS AND THE CASCADE (Entries 22, 27; MODEL). Integration starts at a* = 10⁻³; pumping is
    negligible earlier. Three nested options for the starting tension T_init = ρ_T(a*):
      Option 3   T_init = Λ_coh          dark energy starts at zero ("born on the knife edge")
      Option 1   T_init = 0              dark energy starts at −Λ_coh ("the stars made dark energy")
      Option 2   T_init free (Ω_Tinit)   the Big Bang winds the rope ("starts high"); one extra dial
    Selection, strictly in order: Option 3 if any shape-check grid point passes V1–V4; else Option 1; else
    Option 2; else the dark-energy design fails on design data (the kill switch, Entry 21). Option 2's starting
    tension is read as the winding left by the previous cycle's 0D detonation (Entry 43 §5; UNTESTABLE), which
    enters only through T_init: no new equation or dial.
2.6 SOLVING ONE POINT (Entry 29, the convention both pipelines use).
    - Expanding phase on a fixed fine grid in ln a from a* = 10⁻³ to a = 100. For a fixed E(a), the reservoir has
      the exact solution
        ρ_T(a) = G(a) [T_init + κ ∫_{a*}^{a} S P / (E G) dln a'],   G(a) = exp(−3γ_u ∫_{a*}^{a} dln a' / E)
    - κ is fixed on each pass by ρ_T(1) − Λ_coh = Ω_de,0 (= 1 − Ω_m,0 − Ω_r,0 in the fit; 1 − 0.315 − Ω_r,0 in the
      shape check). κ < 0 means the point fails.
    - Today's comoving dark matter is fixed at Ω_dm,0; earlier values follow from §2.4.
    - a_turn is the root of E²(a) = 0 found by linear interpolation in ln a between the first bracketing pair of
      grid nodes with a > 1 (E² > 0 at the first, ≤ 0 at the second), so it moves continuously with E(a) and the
      iteration cannot flip between two nodes (Entry 47 §8a). If E² ≤ 0 at or before today, the point fails. In the
      shape check, if there is no root by a = 100, a_turn is set to 100 and the point fails V3.
    - LATE TURNAROUNDS IN THE EVIDENCE FIT (solver convention, Entry 46 §4). The evidence fit applies no V3 cut
      (Entry 45 §2), so it meets points that turn around beyond the grid ceiling a = 100. For those the forward
      reach is truncated at the ceiling,
        P_trunc(a) = ∫_a^{100} a'⁻⁴ / E(a') da',
      and convergence is checked over a ≤ 100/1.02. All Round 1 observables and the V4 quantities are evaluated on
      the past light cone, a ≤ 1. The omitted tail is the same constant for every a ≤ 1 and is bounded by
        ΔP_tail ≤ 6.3 × 10⁻⁷ (γ_u Ω_coh)^(−1/3) ≤ 1.4 × 10⁻⁵ anywhere in the prior box             [algebra]
      (the constant is 2^(−2/3) × 10⁻⁶ = 6.2996 × 10⁻⁷; at the box corner γ_u Ω_coh = 10⁻⁴ the bound is
      1.357 × 10⁻⁵; 146_ARGON §A1). Its relative effect on P(a ≤ 1) is at most about 5 × 10⁻⁵, and on Ω_de, E,
      f_dm,excur and f_dm,gross on the past light cone below about 10⁻⁵. The 1.4 × 10⁻⁵ value holds for the
      registered box only; in the doubled boxes of §6.5 the same bound reaches about 1.04 × 10⁻⁴ at the joint
      corner (4.82 × 10⁻⁵ doubling γ_u alone, 2.92 × 10⁻⁵ doubling Ω_coh alone), still far below the data's errors
      [algebra; 197, 199; Entry 47 §8c]. For points with a_turn > 100, t_zero (if
      ρ_de(100) > 0) and t_turn are reported as lower bounds ("beyond a = 100", with the cosmic time at a = 100),
      and the U1 collapse is not integrated for them; no verdict depends on these dates. In the shape check
      nothing changes: no turnaround by a = 100 still fails V3 (Entry 29).
    - Iterate H(a) → a_turn → P(a) → ρ_de → new H(a), starting from ΛCDM with today's Ω_de,0, until the maximum
      relative change of E over a ≤ a_turn/1.02 is below 10⁻⁸ (the last 2% before turnaround is excluded from the
      metric because E → 0 there). At most 50 passes; a point that doesn't converge fails.
2.7 THE SHAPE CHECK (Stage 5.1; stated before any run, Entries 21, 22, 29, 39 §4). A grid scan of the model's own
    equations, before any likelihood is touched. Fixed background (Entry 29; Entry 39 §4): Ω_m,0 = 0.315,
    h = 0.674, Ω_b,0 = 0.0492, Ω_dm,0 = 0.315 − 0.0492 = 0.2658, Ω_r,0 = 9.2 × 10⁻⁵,
    Ω_de,0 = 1 − 0.315 − Ω_r,0 = 0.684908 (144_ARGON B2). Grid: γ_u ∈ [0, 2] (41 points), Ω_coh ∈ [0, 3]
    (31 points), and for Option 2 Ω_Tinit ∈ [0, Ω_coh + 3] (31 points). Each point is solved by §2.6 and passes
    only if all four bands hold:
      V1  ρ_de(today) > 0, and exactly one sign change of dρ_de/dln a (one w = −1 crossing) inside 0.2 ≤ z ≤ 0.8.
          Crossings elsewhere are reported.
      V2  max over the past of ρ_de / ρ_de(today) ≤ 1.15.
      V3  A turnaround by a = 100.
      V4  Both f_dm,excur < 0.05 and f_dm,gross < 0.05, where
            f_dm,excur ≡ max_{a ∈ [10⁻³, 1]} |Ω_dm(a) a³ − Ω_dm,0| / Ω_dm,0
            f_dm,gross ≡ (1/Ω_dm,0) ∫_{10⁻³}^{1} a'³ κ S(a') P(a') / E(a') dln a'
          The net endpoint shift f_dm,net is recorded too. (Requiring both closes the loophole in which pre-peak
          loss and post-peak return cancel in the net.)
    Kill condition: if no point of the cascade-selected option passes, the dark-energy design fails on design
    data and the log records it before any redesign. Reported regardless: w(z) over z ≈ 0.5–1.5 across the viable
    region, f_dm,excur, f_dm,gross and f_dm,net, t_zero and t_turn, and the collapse (§2.8).
    Where V1 and V2 come from: the bands were set from v1's CPL fits on its design data, which included DESI DR2
    (so the check is lightly data-informed; Entry 22 caveat). The v1 CPL posterior medians were w₀ = −0.854,
    w_a = −0.51 (Pantheon+ track; maximum-likelihood best fit −0.859, −0.493) and w₀ = −0.825, w_a = −0.62
    (DES-Dovekie track; best fit −0.831, −0.596), crossing w = −1 at z ≈ 0.40 and 0.39, with dark energy peaking
    about 7.2% and 8.6% above today near z ≈ 0.4 (Entry 43 §2a; the planner's evaluation of the CPL density
    history from v1's frozen fits; medians and best fits from v1's part9_cosmology_results.json, 144_ARGON B3, C2).
    Both fits were flat w₀w_aCDM with v1's priors (§1.3) on the joint likelihood of (i) DESI DR2 BAO with the
    z = 2.33 Lyman-α row replaced by the joint Lyman-α BAO + AP ratios of DESI 2026 (§3.2), (ii) the compressed
    Planck 2018 CMB prior of §3.2, with no separate BBN prior, and (iii) one supernova compilation per track:
    Pantheon+SH0ES (z_HD > 0.01, calibrators excluded, STAT+SYS covariance, absolute magnitude marginalised) or
    DES-Dovekie (STAT+SYS covariance, absolute magnitude marginalised) (v1 PREREG §5.1; 144_ARGON C2).

2.10 THE COMPARISON MODEL CCBH (Entries 30, 31, 46 §7, 47 D14–D16 and §13; T2, debated). Cosmologically coupled
     black holes: dark energy produced by black holes born from stars, so it tracks the star-formation history. It
     is v2's closest published cousin and one of the four compared models. Equations as published in Croker et al.
     2024 (JCAP 10, 094; arXiv:2405.12282v3, e-print SHA-256
     6099b5250cb6c961a9a23e3e93aa3b61f32d9353a26291aa927f825ecef9d87c; Eqs. 2.2 and 2.4–2.7), with the coupling k of
     Farrah et al. 2023 kept free as registered (Entry 30); transcribed by the planner (205) and checked by Argon
     (206). No longer provisional.
       Black holes form from stars at the comoving rate Ξ ψ(a), where ψ is the comoving star-formation rate density
       and Ξ the mass of baryons turned into black holes per unit of stellar mass formed. Each black hole's mass then
       grows as (a/a')^k from its birth at a', with k ≡ −3 w_BH ∈ [0, 3] (k = 0: ordinary black holes; k = 3:
       constant physical density once formed, w = −1).
       Baryons (2405.12282 Eq. 2.2), with ω_b^proj the early-universe ("projected") baryon density:
         ρ_b(a) = ρ_crit,0 ω_b^proj h⁻² a⁻³                                         for a < a_i
         ρ_b(a) = ρ_crit,0 ω_b^proj h⁻² a⁻³ − (Ξ/a³) ∫_{a_i}^{a} ψ(a') da' / (a' H(a'))   for a ≥ a_i
         equivalently ρ̇_b + 3H ρ_b = −a⁻³ Ξ ψ(a).
       Dark energy (2405.12282 Eq. 2.4 with 1 + w = 1 − k/3):
         dρ_DE/da + [(3 − k)/a] ρ_DE = Ξ ψ(a) / (H(a) a⁴),   ρ_DE(a ≤ a_i) = 0,
         so  ρ_DE(a) = a^(k − 3) ∫_{a_i}^{a} Ξ [ψ(a') / (a' H(a'))] a'^(−k) da'.
       At k = 3 this is the paper's Eq. 2.5, dρ_DE/da = Ξ ψ / (H a⁴) [algebra; 205, 206].
       Expansion: H² = (8πG/3)[ρ_b + ρ_γ + ρ_ν + ρ_DE + ρ_c], flat, with ρ_c ∝ a⁻³ and neutrinos as in §2.1's
       evidence-fit convention (the same as the paper's: N_eff = 3.044, one 0.06 eV species).
     PARAMETERS (Entry 47 §13). The four sampled parameters are (Ω_m, ω_b, h, k) of §1.3, read for CCBH as
     ω_b ≡ ω_b^proj and Ω_m ≡ Ω_m^proj = (ω_c + ω_b^proj + ω_ν)/h². The compressed CMB term (§3.2) uses these early
     values (Ω_m^eff = Ω_m^proj). Ξ is not sampled: it is solved at each evaluation from flatness today,
       Ω_DE(1) − ΔΩ_b(1) = (Ξ/ρ_crit,0) ∫_{a_i}^{1} [ψ(a') / (a' H(a'))] (a'^(−k) − 1) da' = 1 − Ω_m^proj − Ω_r,0,
     where ΔΩ_b(1) is today's baryon loss. Today's baryon density s ω_b^proj and the survival fraction

     k = 3, sample Ξ and derive H₀). At k = 3 it is the same model with a different prior, disclosed.

4.1 MECHANISM (MODEL; [G] fading-branch picture). Two ingredients: (a) a conserved, cored cloud of loose frays,
    ρ_dm, the same dark matter as §2, exchanged only with ρ_T as in §2.4; (b) the pull of fresh tangent branches of
    our own matter, born at the bow wave of the Now in step with our string, which pull alongside it until they
    fall out of step. Branches are born at Γ_birth = K a₀/c and fade at Γ_fade = K g_tot/c, so the number in step
    is N − 1 = a₀/g_tot; K cancels from every observable. A faded branch stops pulling and deposits nothing into
    our ρ_dm. Births form a Poisson train whose fluctuations average to the smooth law. Fading is instant (Regime
    τ-2, far below one second). A stationary interference pattern between strings is EXPLORATORY only.
4.2 THE GRAVITY RULE (MODEL, chosen on Gregory's written authorisation; Entry 41 §3). The AQUAL field equation of
    Bekenstein & Milgrom (1984, ApJ 286, 7), which conserves momentum, angular momentum and energy:
      ∇·[μ(|∇Φ|/a₀) ∇Φ] = 4πG (ρ_b + ρ_dm),   μ(x) = x/(1 + x)
    The boost acts on all the matter a branch carries, ordinary matter and cloud alike. In spherical symmetry the
    exact solution is g_tot = ν(g_N/a₀) g_N with ν(y) = ½ + √(¼ + 1/y), which gives the radial acceleration relation
    in its simple-ν form and the baryonic Tully–Fisher relation.                                      [Std/T2]
    QUMOND may be used only as a computing shortcut where it coincides with AQUAL (spherical symmetry) or where the
    difference has been checked and bounded (Stage 3.3d). RULES: light bending follows the same potential as
    matter (Φ = Ψ), and gravitational waves travel at c (§5.2).
4.3 BACKGROUND-DECELERATION RULE (MODEL, fixed before any test; Entry 41 §4). g_tot includes the universe's own
    deceleration across the system's size, |ä/a| L. For a bound galaxy or cluster, L = r from its centre. For a
    linear perturbation of comoving wavenumber k, L = a/k and g_tot = (g_bg² + g_pec²)^½. At recombination this is
    4.4–6.0 × 10⁴ a₀ on 100–135 kpc scales, a boost of about 2 × 10⁻⁵; across k = 0.01–0.2 Mpc⁻¹ the boost there is
    2.6 × 10⁻⁵ to 5.3 × 10⁻⁴ [algebra; 071_ARGON §2b]. For 2–5 kpc scales the boost switches on at 1 + z ≈ 100–134.
    At late times the term is negligible on galaxy and cluster scales.
4.4 THE ACCELERATION SCALE (MODEL RULE, with disclosures; Entry 41 §6):
      a₀ = (cH₀/2π) √Ω_coh
    read as one branch born per cycle of the bow's baseline beat. Disclosures: H_coh is an expansion rate in
    DE-F2, so reading it as a beat rate is a rule, not a derivation; the near-match a₀ ≈ cH₀/2π was known before
    the factor was chosen (charter R5); 1/2π and 1/6 differ by 4.7%. Testable content: a₀ = 1.2 × 10⁻¹⁰ m s⁻²
    requires the DE-F2 fit to land on Ω_coh ≈ 1.2–1.3 (1.33 at H₀ = 67.4, 1.23 at H₀ = 70). The galaxy side is
    tested twice: once with one universal a₀ fitted to the galaxies, once with a₀ predicted from the DE-F2 fit
    and no galaxy dial; the cross-check is whether they agree.
    THE CROSS-CHECK IS DECIDING (D10, Entry 47 §3). Predicted: ln a₀,pred = ln(cH₀/2π) + ½ ln Ω_coh on each Run 2
    posterior sample (that sample's H₀ and Ω_coh). Fitted: ln a₀,fit, the posterior of the free-a₀ SPARC fit
    (Stage 5.4). With Δ = mean(ln a₀,pred) − mean(ln a₀,fit) and σ_comb = √(var(ln a₀,pred) + var(ln a₀,fit)),
    means and variances over each posterior:
      AGREE (pass) if |Δ| ≤ 2σ_comb;   FAIL if |Δ| > 3σ_comb;   TENSION between.
    Log space because both priors are log-uniform and the relation is multiplicative; means and variances because
    the variance of a difference of independent quantities is the sum of their variances for any posterior shape.

    a FAIL on either counts. Disclosure: Entry 43 §2 estimated that survivors may sit at Ω_coh ≈ 1.3–3, while

4.5 THE CLOUD (MODEL; Entry 41 §7). One universal core radius R_core, set on design data (Stage 5.4; prior §6).
    Every bound system carries the cosmic share, M_cloud = (Ω_dm/Ω_b) M_b ≈ 5.36 M_b, an explicit model
    assumption with no dial. Cored Plummer profile, centred on the system's ordinary matter (in a merger, on each
    subcluster's galaxies):
      ρ_dm(r) = (3 M_cloud / 4π R_core³) (1 + r²/R_core²)^(−5/2)
      M_dm(<r) = M_cloud r³ / (r² + R_core²)^(3/2)
      Σ_dm(R) = M_cloud / [π R_core² (1 + R²/R_core²)²]                                                  [Std]
    Because the cloud's share inside r depends only on r/R_core, dwarfs measured inside about 10 kpc are barely
    affected (about 0.2% at r = 10 kpc for R_core = 110 kpc; 071_ARGON §2c).
    Consequences recorded before any data was examined: the cloud's own field is boosted, so outer rotation
    curves feel it (for M_b = 5 × 10¹⁰ M☉ and R_core = 110 kpc, about 5 / 11 / 19% extra pull at 30 / 40 / 50 kpc;
    about 1 / 2 / 4% for R_core = 200 kpc); the radial acceleration and Tully–Fisher relations hold unchanged only
    where the enclosed cloud is small compared with M_b; at weak-lensing radii the pull rises to about
    √(1 + 5.36) ≈ 2.5 times the ordinary-matter value, a step rather than a bump.
4.6 CLUSTERS (Entry 41 §5). Handled by their own high total pull (g_tot above a₀) plus their clouds, with no
    cluster dial. Superfluid dark matter, temperature or velocity cutoffs and a "no negative frays" floor are all
    out.
4.7 ROPE STRUCTURE (Entry 41 §9; UNTESTABLE, no term in any equation). Strings exchange between neighbouring
    strands inside a rope; taut frays hold tension (ρ_T) and slack frays are the loose frays (ρ_dm); nothing crosses
    the gap between ropes, which is irreversible decoherence, not a force (consistent with no-signalling between
    branches, Polchinski 1991). Strings are equal in size. The 7 × 19 cable is the picture of the structure; the
    133 and 19 counts are not inputs.

8. INTERPRETATION AND THE UNTESTABLE BIN (never scored; no equation changes)
==========================================================================================
8.1 THE TIME-REVERSED CONTRACTION (Entries 18–20). After turnaround the Now runs backward and unmakes history:
    unanswered offers return to their senders, entropy runs downhill (the cycle's entropy reset), and the reversal
    ends at the start state. Tier 2 history: Gold's reversing arrow; Hawking 1985; withdrawn after Page and Laflamme.
    Known gap: entropy carried across the cycle (Tolman's problem) is asserted to reset, not derived.
8.2 ONE CABLE, ONE TURNAROUND (Entry 39 §5). Coupled ropes phase-lock like metronomes, so every rope reverses
    together; this is stated as a model assumption that our universe's densities are typical of the cable average.
    Dead universes, whose star formation has ended, drain toward the negative floor and act as slack weight
    ("negative strain") that slows the cable until every rope reverses at once.
8.3 THE ENDS OF THE CABLE MEET (Entries 37, 39 §5). The collapse returns every rope to the single 0D point, where all
    matter across the cable touches itself at once and detonates, birthing the next cable. The "same matter touching
    itself explodes" rule is a fiction convention (12 Monkeys, Timecop), presented as the model's image only. Tier 2
    rhymes: the ekpyrotic and cyclic models (Khoury, Ovrut, Steinhardt & Turok 2001; Steinhardt & Turok 2002);
    Penrose's conformal cyclic cosmology.
8.4 WHAT OPTION 2'S STARTING TENSION MEANS (Entry 43 §5). The detonation is the moment of most interaction and most
    forward reach, so it winds the newborn rope; tension wound then costs almost nothing from the loose-fray supply.
    It enters only as T_init. The model doesn't say how much the burst winds, so T_init stays free; a rule carrying
    the end-of-cycle tension through the bounce is a candidate for a later version.
8.5 THE RATE OF TIME (Entry 40 §1). A clock rate tied to dark energy stays Gregory's idea and a candidate test only;
    DE-F2 contains no such term.
8.6 ROPE STRUCTURE AND THE GAP BETWEEN ROPES: §4.7. PARKED: the "two waves" carry-over of dark matter from the
    previous crunch (Entry 41 §10). An interference pattern between strings is EXPLORATORY (§4.1).

9. PRE-RUN ESTIMATES, DISCLOSED (none sets a rule; the registered runs decide)

These were worked out before any model output, by algebra on the frozen equations and bands, or by rough
spherical approximations. They are disclosed because the people who fixed the tests knew them.
9.1 PROPERTIES OF THE INPUTS (Entries 20, 39 §1): S(a) peaks at z = 1.86 at 8.9 times today; from z = 2 to today,
    S falls about 8.9 times and P about 12.9 times for ΛCDM-like expansion.
9.2 THE SHAPE CHECK, EXPECTED OUTCOME (Entry 43 §2) [algebra, with stated simplifications]:
    (a) V4 caps the late bump: a smooth bump peaking at z = 0.4 passes V4's excursion part only if it stays below
        about 4% above today; the v1 CPL curves (peaks 7.2% and 8.6%) would give f_dm,excur of 13.7% and 16.3%.
    (b) V3 needs a minimum drain: turning around by a = 100 requires 3γ_u Ω_T,0 ≳ 0.082–0.123 (a strict lower bound).
    (c) V3 and V4 pull against each other: D_past + Δf_fill = f_dm,gross + |Δf_dm(a_peak)| < 0.10, with the past
        drain implied by (b) already about 9–12% of Ω_dm,0.
    (d) Options 3 and 1 are expected to fail V4 (filling costs Δf_fill ≈ 11–38% of Ω_dm,0).
    (e) Option 2 is the only expected candidate, and marginal: gross inflow roughly 13–18% of Ω_dm,0 for a peak at
        z = 0.4, falling to roughly 4–5% (3.8–5.3%) at z = 0.8, so any survivors are expected near z_peak ≈ 0.65–0.80
        with Ω_coh ≈ 1.3–3, and Option 2 may miss V4 narrowly, which would trigger the kill switch.
    (f) If v2 survives, its dark energy peaks earlier (z ≈ 0.65–0.8) and more gently (a few percent) than the
        DESI-style hint (z ≈ 0.4, 7–9%): a sharper, more distinctive prediction, not a match to the hint.
    Gregory's decision: no response in the design (Entry 43 §3).
9.3 CMB SENSITIVITY TO THE DRIFT (Entry 42 §6) [algebra; properties of the CMB prior]: about 1.6σ in R per 1%
    recombination-to-today drift at fixed D_M(z*); about 1.6–2.3σ per 1% after profiling over Ω_m,0 and h. Run 2's
    CMB term alone narrows the drift to σ(δ_dm) ≈ 0.45–0.60% (128_ARGON B2). The Stage 4.3 power check replaces
    these with fake-universe results.
9.4 GALAXIES AND CLUSTERS (Entries 41 §7, 44 §7). Outer-curve extra pull for M_b = 5 × 10¹⁰ M☉: about 5 / 11 / 19%
    at 30 / 40 / 50 kpc for R_core = 110 kpc, about 1 / 2 / 4% for R_core = 200 kpc. Rough spherical estimates made
    in thread 120 (Argon A15): DF2's enclosed cloud and total effective mass at 2.7, 3.1 and 7.6 kpc for
    R_core = 50–200 kpc fell inside T5c's pass range in those approximations; a generic rich cluster at 1 Mpc came
    out at about 9 times its ordinary matter, about 40% above typical observed ratios. They were not extended, and no
    Bullet Cluster estimate was made. T-d2 is exploratory for the reasons the paper itself states, and a lower bound
    couldn't sink a heavy prediction in any case.
9.5 THE EVIDENCE TEST'S TUNING COST (Entry 45 §7) [algebra]: any V1–V4 window in γ_u is about Δγ_u ≲ 0.1 γ_min
    wide (γ_min ≈ 0.008–0.02), or empty. That window alone would cost about 7–8 nats under a flat prior on [0, 2],
    about 4.4 nats under log-uniform [10⁻³, 2], and about 4.0 under log-uniform [γ_min, 2]. Under S6, the κ ≥ 0 and
    V4 cuts force Option 2's Ω_Tinit into a slice about 0.08–0.11 wide, about 3.8–4.1 nats, so a ΛCDM-like corner
    with no likelihood gain would sit near ln B ≈ −4.5 to −5.0, at the sink line. To clear it, v2 has to fit the data
    better than ΛCDM. S6 is probably kinder to v2 than S5 (dropping V3's deadline removes the forced drain); it was
    chosen on the principles of §6.3.

10. WHAT V2 DOES NOT TEST

10.1 GROWTH OF STRUCTURE. v2 has no structure-formation code and no Boltzmann code with its own dark sector and
     gravity boost. Its cosmology is tested through the background expansion and the compressed CMB parameters only;
     the full CMB power spectrum, cluster abundance, the reionization optical depth and the small-scale Lyman-α
     forest are not predicted (the last three are reported, §5.5). These are the likeliest places v2 fails outside
     this trial.
10.2 THE RELATIVISTIC COMPLETION. v2 has no relativistic action of its own; c_T = c and Φ = Ψ are stated rules
     (§5.2).
10.3 THE STATIC SHARE RATIO. v2 makes no claim on today's dark-to-ordinary ratio (§3.1).
10.4 THE ORIGIN OF a₀. The beat-rate reading of a₀ = (cH₀/2π)√Ω_coh is a rule, not a derivation; only its consequence,
     the agreement of the two a₀ routes, is tested (§4.4).
10.5 THE FAR FUTURE AND THE CYCLE. The dates t_zero and t_turn are stated predictions no data can check; the
     collapse, the reversal and the restart are UNTESTABLE (§8).
